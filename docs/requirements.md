# Requirements

## Functional requirements 
1. The system shall present visual keyword cards of selected words with their verified meanings from an approved source.

2. The system shall show verified meanings and then provide textually or semantically related meanings and relationships to selected Quranic words and verses.

3. The system shall use a Large Language Model (LLM) to generate proposed memorization learning cues based on provided, verified source material.

4.The system shall offer memorization practice activities and display feedback to the user whether the provided word/verse sequence is correct or incorrect.

5. The system shall offer interactive activities in which children can arrange words or parts of verses of the Quran in order.


## Non-functional requirements 
1. The system shall present a selected memorization activity within 3 seconds in at least 95 % of 20 test runs under documented test conditions.

2. The system shall present the same text of the Quran that has been verified by the designated source with no discrepancies found in the entire set of defined test Quran verses.

3. The system shall complete a word-sequencing task without support, based on the same standard instructions for 4 out of 5 representative primary school children.
