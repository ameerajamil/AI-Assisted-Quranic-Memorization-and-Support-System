# AI-Assisted Quranic Memorization and Support System

An AI-assisted system that explores visual and meaning-based learning support to help primary school children memorize Quranic verses.

## Problem statement
The primary school children may find it difficult to remember the Quranic verses, especially the order in which the words are meant to be presented and the sequence in which similar words are meant to be pronounced. In addition, some children may have difficulties seeing the verses in their mind and making links between them when they are memorizing. It can be difficult for children to maintain their attention and motivation when it comes to repeated recitation and revision. Hence, the current project aims to investigate the role of the visual keyword cards and interactive memorization exercises in helping children to memorize the Quran.
